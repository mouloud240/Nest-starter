# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Backend engineers who evaluate or adopt `create-nest-starter`. They arrive from
search, GitHub, or the CLI's post-scaffold hint. They want a production-grade
NestJS baseline without assembling boilerplate by hand, and they judge the
project in seconds.

## Product Purpose

Landing page and docs for `create-nest-starter`: a command-line tool that
scaffolds a production-ready NestJS backend. Success is a visitor understanding
what it generates and running `npx create-nest-starter` within 60 seconds.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, technical, confident, restrained. A premium developer tool that
takes its craft seriously.

## Anti-references

- Typical SaaS landing pages with grids of small icon cards.
- Feature-grid sections and gradient-on-everything decoration.
- Beige / cream / "warm" backgrounds.
- Generic "modern developer tool" blue.

## Design Principles

- Typography is the interface. Scale, weight, and whitespace carry the message
  before any decoration.
- Color is atmosphere, not accent. The brand red lives in the hero environment
  and interactive glow, never sprayed across every component.
- Restraint reads as expertise. One dominant idea per fold; nothing decorative
  without a reason.
- Technical detail is semantic, not costume. Monospace is for labels, versions,
  and machine facts only.
- Content must stay readable on a dark, near-black stage with strong contrast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast for body text on the dark stage.
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support — no non-essential motion for users who
  opt out.
- Keyboard-usable nav and focus-visible outlines.
- Meaningful alt text on all images and icons.
